THAWING MEMORY
VISUAL DIRECTION AND DESIGN TOKENS

Status: Initial direction for implementation
Version: 1.0
Date: 31 August 2026


1. VISUAL INTENTION

Thawing Memory should feel quiet, domestic, reflective, and provisional. It should not resemble a chatbot, recipe database, productivity dashboard, or cultural tourism website.

The interface should support slow reading and deliberate correction. User-authored content must feel primary. AI content must remain legible but visually quieter and clearly labelled.

This direction is a working foundation. Colours, proportions, and component treatments may change when they are tested in the complete interaction.


2. CULTURAL APPROACH

The visual language should be Kerala-informed without presenting a generic or decorative idea of Indian culture.

The initial palette draws from restrained domestic materials and environments:

- Coconut cream for the main background
- Paper white for readable surfaces
- Clay red for primary actions
- Leaf green for secondary and reflective states
- Sand for borders and quiet separation
- Dark brown ink instead of pure black

The design should avoid using visual shorthand such as mandalas, spice collages, ornamental scripts, temple imagery, or decorative patterns without a specific relationship to the project.

Illustration, if added later, should support the Kitchen as a remembered domestic space rather than attempt to represent one authentic Kerala home.


3. AUTHORSHIP SYSTEM

User-authored content uses the warm user surface token.

AI-generated content uses the pale green AI surface token.

Uncertainty and limitation notes use the muted sand uncertainty surface.

Authorship must never rely on colour alone. Every area will also use persistent text labels such as:

- Your memory
- Your version
- Your family correction
- Provisional AI reflection
- Why this may be incomplete
- AI question


4. TYPOGRAPHY

Display type uses Georgia with Times New Roman as a fallback. The serif face introduces a reflective and editorial quality without requiring a remote font download.

Body and interface type use Aptos, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial, and the system sans-serif fallback chain. This keeps forms, longer passages, and controls familiar and readable across devices.

The type scale ranges from 12 pixels for small labels to a responsive landing title that grows from approximately 40 to 88 pixels.

6. SPACING AND SHAPE

The spacing system uses a four-pixel base and expands through 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, and 96 pixels.

Small controls use restrained corners. Cards and reflective panels use softer 12 to 24 pixel corners. Fully rounded shapes are reserved for small status labels rather than every component.

Shadows should remain warm and subtle. Borders and spacing should establish hierarchy before stronger shadows are introduced.


7. ACCESSIBILITY RULES

- Main text must retain strong contrast against cream and paper surfaces.
- Keyboard focus uses a distinct blue token that is not otherwise part of the brand palette.
- Status and authorship are communicated through labels and structure as well as colour.
- Body text should not be smaller than 16 pixels.
- Controls inherit the body font and must remain readable when zoomed.
- Motion must remain brief and nonessential.
- The layout must work from a 320-pixel viewport upward.
